**Course Introduction**:
This course delves into UAV architectural frameworks and operational principles, emphasizing *ROS*-based control systems. Using *XTDrone*, students develop autonomous perception, control, and decision-making algorithms through simulation. Focused on *Crazyflie* UAVs, it advances swarm algorithm design and real-world validation via a "theory-simulation-experimentation" triad, equipping learners with technical foundations for UAV intelligent control and collaborative swarm applications.

**Responsibilities**:
